Questions owners
ask first.

What if two agents accept at the same moment?

The first accept wins and is connected. The others see that the call was taken, and stay available.

Is there a round-robin option?

No. Every eligible agent sees the call at the same moment, and the first to accept takes it.

What does the caller hear while it rings?

A short connecting message, then ringback. If nobody accepts within 40 seconds, they can leave a message.

Are calls recorded, and who can listen?

Yes, both sides of every connected call. Your agency can play back any recording from the call’s record.

What if the call drops before the plan’s length?

Nothing is charged and the number stays hidden. The agent can recover the contact for the plan price until midnight that day.

Can an agent take two calls at once?

Not by default. An agent on a call is offered nothing else until it ends.
