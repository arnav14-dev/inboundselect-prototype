How a listing is born.

From a call nobody could take to a conversation that can still become a sale.

01

Nobody free within 40 seconds

The call was offered to every eligible agent at once. When none of them accepts inside the window, the caller isn’t left listening to a ringing line.

02

The caller leaves a message

They say who they are, what they need and when to call. The message is recorded in full.

03

It’s listed on your agency’s board

With the recording, the caller’s state, the product and the time they rang. Only your own agents can see it.

04

An agent takes it and calls back

They listen first, take it at your agency’s price, then ring the caller back while the need is still fresh.

05

It comes off the board at 90 days

The price steps down as the message ages. After 90 days it is removed for good.
