What you control.

We run the routing. Every decision that shapes your business stays with you.

01

Numbers

Run calls through your own Twilio account, or let us manage one for you. Your adverts and call vendors keep pointing at numbers you control.

02

Products and scripts

Final expense, Medicare and life. Write a script for each product, and it stays on the agent’s screen for the whole call, so every caller hears the same story.

03

States

Your agents’ licences decide who can take which call. A caller from Georgia is only offered to agents licensed in Georgia, and nobody else sees the call.

04

Acceptance plans

You set the length and the price agents pay: 60 seconds for $35, for example. A call that ends sooner costs the agent nothing.

05

Call handling rules

Decide how each of your lines behaves: which product it rings as, and which of your agents can see its calls. Change it whenever you like.

06

Refund decisions

Agents can ask for a refund within 48 hours of a charge. Every request comes to you with the call recording, and you decide.
