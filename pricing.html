Your side of the money

What your agents
pay you.

You set it: a monthly amount, a per-call acceptance plan, or both. Agent payments go to your own Stripe account, and Inbound Select takes nothing from them.

  • An agent is charged only when talk time reaches the plan’s seconds.
  • The caller’s number stays hidden until the call is billed.
  • Agents can ask for a refund within 48 hours. You review every request.

How billing works.

Four rules, in the order you’ll meet them. Nothing else is tucked between them.

01

Charged on approval

We take your card when you apply, and charge it only once your agency is approved.

02

Fees at $100, or Monday

Connection fees build up as calls connect. We collect them once they reach $100, or every Monday.

03

Month to month

There is no annual contract. Each month is billed on its own.

04

Callback sales, 75 / 25

When an agent buys a Callback Request, 75% of the sale goes to your agency and 25% to the platform. The sale stays inside your agency.

Questions, answered.

Something else? Write to support@inboundselect.com

What counts as a connected call?

A call with 15 seconds or more of talk time. Only connected calls carry the $2.50 fee.

Do agents pay Inbound Select anything?

No. Agents pay your agency, at prices you set, into your own Stripe account. We take nothing from those payments.

Can I add my own brand later?

Yes. Both plans are month to month, so you can move to Agency with your own brand when you’re ready to put your name, colours and web address on it.

What if a call is shorter than 15 seconds?

There’s no connection fee. A call only counts as connected once it reaches 15 seconds of talk.

Do agents beyond 50 cost anything?

No. Agents 11 to 50 are $15 each per month. From the 51st agent on, there is no per-agent charge.

Is there an annual plan?

No. Both plans are month to month, with no annual contract.
