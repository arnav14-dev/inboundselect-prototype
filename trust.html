Every call, on the record.

Four questions owners ask about their callers, with the same answer at every agency, every time.

01

Who can hear a call?

Your agency. Both sides of every call are recorded, and you can play any of them back from the call’s record. Agents see only their own calls.

02

Who sees the caller’s number?

Your agency always does. The agent who took the call sees it only once the call is billed; until then it stays masked.

03

Who decides a refund?

Your agency. An agent can ask within 48 hours of a charge, and every request comes to you to review.

04

Who can be offered a call?

Only agents your agency has approved and who are licensed in the caller’s state. Nobody else sees it ring.

Two roles. Clear lines.

There are two kinds of account, and nothing in between. Each one sees what it needs, and nothing more.

The owner or admin

Agency

  • Sees everything: every number, call, recording, agent and charge.
  • Sets the plans and prices its agents pay.
  • Approves every agent, and reviews every refund request.

The licensed agent

Agent

  • Sees their own calls and their own network, never another agent’s.
  • Is offered calls only from states they’re licensed in.
  • Pays the agency directly, through Stripe.

Privacy

Privacy, in plain words.

What we store
Account details, call records, call recordings and transactions.

Your agency sees everything in its account. Each agent sees their own calls and their own network.
Recordings
Stored with the call they belong to, for your agency to play back.
