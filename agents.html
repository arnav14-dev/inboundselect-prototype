On the other end

The person on the line chose to ring.

They saw an advert or a letter, picked up the phone and asked to talk about cover. Nobody is surprised to hear from you. Your job is to listen well.

Before your first shift.

Anything else is set by your agency, so they are the best people to ask.

Do I need my own licence?

Yes. You need an active licence in each state you take calls from, and you’re only ever offered calls from those states.

Who sets the prices?

Your agency. It sets your subscription and the acceptance plans, such as 30 seconds for $20 or 60 seconds for $35. You see the price before you accept.

Can I pause?

Yes, with one switch. Calls stop being offered to you until you switch it back on. Your clients and history stay exactly where they are.

What if the caller hangs up early?

If the call ends before your plan’s length, you’re not charged. You can still recover the caller’s number for the plan price until midnight that day.

How do I join?

Through your agency’s invite, or a referral code from an agent already on its team. Once you’re approved and your wallet covers your plan plus the $10 buffer, you can set yourself available.
